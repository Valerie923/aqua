# Demo video storyboard (90 seconds)

Goal: the judges must see the AI catch a wrong answer **before the 45-second mark**.
Record on a phone (or a phone-sized browser window), portrait. No narration of features
before they are shown; show first, name second.

| Time | Screen | What happens | Voice-over (short) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | Title card | "StreamCheck — an AI second pair of eyes for citizen stream assessments" | "Citizen science data is only as good as the answers. This is a second pair of eyes." |
| 0:05–0:12 | Site + Photos | Pick "Sungei Ulu Pandan", tap **Or try demo photos → concrete canal**. Header shows "AI is reading your photos…". | "Same photos, same questions as the OneAquaHealth app. While I answer, the AI reads the photos." |
| 0:12–0:25 | Section A | Answer fast. Deliberately tap **Bottom type: Natural** on the concrete canal. Green ticks appear on answers that match. | "I answer as usual. Green tick means the AI agrees." |
| 0:25–0:40 | **Quick check** | The flag: *"Our AI thinks the bottom type looks like 'Artificial' because the bed is smooth grey concrete. You answered 'Natural'. Keep your answer or change it?"* Pause on it. Tap **Change**. | "Here it caught me. It says why, in plain words. I decide — and my decision is recorded." |
| 0:40–0:50 | Section B/C | Tap "Not sure" on water aspect; Quick check offers the AI's reading with evidence. Accept. | "When I'm not sure, it offers a suggestion instead of a guess." |
| 0:50–1:00 | Section D | Show the **Our suggestion** card (rule-based, with reasons) then pick the overall assessment. | "Before I rate the stream, it shows what my own answers add up to." |
| 1:00–1:15 | Review | Scroll: reliability score with breakdown, **What we checked** audit trail, **One Health** card (mosquito / pollution / biodiversity). | "Every submission gets a reliability score, an audit trail of what was checked, and a One Health summary." |
| 1:15–1:25 | Done | Tap **Export FHIR**, then **Send to FHIR sandbox**; show the returned IDs. | "And it exports as HL7 FHIR, so validated data can flow into health systems." |
| 1:25–1:30 | Submissions map | Map with coloured sites, then end card with the live link. | "Human judgement, kept. Data quality, up. Live link below." |

Tips
- Practice the Section A answers so the "wrong" one is the only slow tap.
- If the AI does not flag the answer you expected, don't fake it: pick another field it did flag. The point is that the flag is real.
- Keep the phone in portrait; judges score usability on the phone experience.
